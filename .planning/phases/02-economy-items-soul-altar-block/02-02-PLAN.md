## Phase Goal

**As a** necromancer player, **I want to** craft a Harvester, reap villagers into Soul
Fragments and Soul Blocks, and place a Soul Altar that accepts a socketed Soul Block, **so
that** the tangible soul economy exists and behaves correctly before any binding GUI is built.

<slice>
**This slice:** the Harvester reaps villager souls. After this plan a player can hold a
Harvester, hit any villager once, and get exactly one Soul Fragment — the one genuinely
custom mechanic of the phase, end to end and covered by a GREEN GameTest. The Harvester is a
bad general weapon (~3-4 damage, no sweep). Wandering traders and zombie villagers are
untouched by the soul-reap.

Not in this slice: crafting the Harvester (recipe is plan 02-03), the Soul Block, the altar.
</slice>

<key_decisions>
- **Instakill hook (D-08 / Discretion):** game-bus `LivingDamageEvent.Pre` (post-mitigation) —
  `setNewDamage(health + absorption + 1)` is unconditionally lethal past armor / Resistance V /
  absorption. NOT `LivingIncomingDamageEvent` (pre-mitigation — Resistance V can survive
  `MAX_VALUE`). Per `02-RESEARCH.md` Architecture Pattern 1 + Anti-Patterns.
- **Fragment drop (D-10 / Discretion):** separate `LivingDropsEvent` handler —
  `event.getDrops().clear()` then add exactly one Fragment `ItemEntity`. NOT `LivingDeathEvent`.
- **"Killed by Harvester" detection:** `DamageSource#getWeaponItem()` (vanilla weapon snapshot).
  Fallback if absent on 21.1.248 (A1): `src.getEntity() instanceof Player p &&
  p.getMainHandItem().getItem() instanceof HarvesterItem`.
- **Target scope (D-09):** `target instanceof net.minecraft.world.entity.npc.Villager` exactly —
  includes babies; excludes `WanderingTrader` and `ZombieVillager`. NOT `AbstractVillager`.
- **Phase 6 seam:** leave a documented comment where `&& !((Villager) target).hasData(EMPLOYEE)`
  slots in (ECON-04 — Phase 6). Do not implement it.
</key_decisions>
