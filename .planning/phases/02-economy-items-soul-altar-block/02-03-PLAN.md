## Phase Goal

**As a** necromancer player, **I want to** craft a Harvester, reap villagers into Soul
Fragments and Soul Blocks, and place a Soul Altar that accepts a socketed Soul Block, **so
that** the tangible soul economy exists and behaves correctly before any binding GUI is built.

<slice>
**This slice:** the soul economy becomes tangible and discoverable. After this plan a player
can craft the Harvester at a table, turn 4 Fragments into a Soul Block and back, see all of
it in JEI/EMI, and experience the guided discovery chain: pick up an emerald → "Necromantic
Apprentice" toast unlocks the Harvester recipe; get your first Fragment → "First Harvest"
toast unlocks the Soul Block recipe. The placed Soul Block is a real, glowing block.

Not in this slice: the Soul Altar block, its recipe, and the third advancement
("Soul Mason" → altar recipe) — those are plan 02-04.
</slice>

<key_decisions>
- **Datagen (D-17): hand-write all JSON.** `net.minecraft.data.recipes.RecipeProvider` forces an
  `unlockedBy(...)` criterion and auto-generates a `advancement/recipes/<ns>/<path>.json` that
  unlocks the recipe on ingredient pickup — which defeats the D-15 gating chain. Hand-writing the
  ~18 small JSON files keeps full control and needs no second run configuration while the phase is
  also introducing the first `BlockEntity` and first renderer. Revisit datagen at Phase 3 (first
  `MenuType` + real blockstate variants). Full analysis: `02-RESEARCH.md` Datagen Decision.
- **Advancement trigger for step 2 (D-16):** `minecraft:inventory_changed` on
  `secondshift:soul_fragment` — simplest and robust, and the Fragment's sole source is a harvest.
- **NO `data/secondshift/advancement/recipes/*.json`** — the deliberate omission is what makes the
  recipe unknown until its gating advancement grants it (RESEARCH Assumption A8 — verify in a
  fresh creative world that `secondshift:harvester` is absent from the recipe book before the
  advancement).
- **1.21.1 JSON shapes:** recipe result is `{"id": ..., "count": ...}` (not `{"item": ...}`);
  item predicates use `"items": [...]`. Validate every file against a known-good vanilla
  `data/minecraft/recipe/*.json` / `data/minecraft/advancement/*.json` from the 1.21.1 client jar
  before finalizing (A7).
</key_decisions>
