<objective>
Fix two independent, already-researched bugs (no re-investigation needed — both are fully scoped in `.planning/REVIEW-phases-1-4.md` CR-01 and `.planning/research/entity-attachment-sync-timing-investigation.md`):

1. **CR-01:** `EmployeeManager.bind()` spawns the employee villager at `altarPos.above()` — the exact `BlockPos` the bind precondition (`ProfessionResolver.fromAbove`) requires a job-site block to occupy. Every real bind spawns the villager overlapping the job-site block.
2. **Sync timing bug:** `ClientEmployeeSyncDebug` checks `villager.hasData(EMPLOYEE)` synchronously inside the client `EntityJoinLevelEvent` handler, but NeoForge bundles the attachment-sync sub-packet strictly after the spawn packet in the same `ClientboundBundlePacket` — `hasData()` is guaranteed false at that point, every time. This makes the diagnostic permanently silent, not actually informative.

Purpose: unblock reliable employee binding (the mod's core value loop) and make the sync diagnostic actually able to report success or real failure.
Output: corrected spawn position + regression GameTest; a poll-based client-tick diagnostic that can't structurally miss the sync.
</objective>
